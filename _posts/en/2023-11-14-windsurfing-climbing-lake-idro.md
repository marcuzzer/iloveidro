## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the wind conditions like for windsurfing at Lake Idro?**
Lake Idro is renowned for the Ander wind, a reliable thermal wind from the north that blows regularly in the afternoon, especially in summer. It offers ideal and predictable conditions for windsurfing and kitesurfing.

**Are there via ferrata routes near Lake Idro?**
Yes, the most well-known routes are the Ferrata delle Sasse and the Ferrata del Crènch, both in the surrounding mountains. They offer various difficulty levels with spectacular views.

**Can you windsurf and climb on the same day?**
Absolutely. Mornings are perfect for climbing (cool air, dry rock), while the afternoon Ander wind makes it ideal windsurfing time.
