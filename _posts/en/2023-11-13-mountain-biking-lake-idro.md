Lake Idro is an ideal destination for mountain biking enthusiasts. With a variety of routes offered by [Greenway Valli Resilienti](https://www.greenwayvalliresilienti.it/it/percorsi/), there's always a new adventure waiting to be discovered.

![Mountain Bike Trail](/assets/img/path-to-mtb-trail-photo.jpg)
*One of the many breathtaking mountain bike trails around Lake Idro*

### The Best MTB Routes

Here are some of the best mountain biking routes I've found on the Greenway Valli Resilienti website:

- **Lake Loop**: An easy and scenic route that hugs the shoreline, suitable for all levels, spanning 30 km.
- **Monte Stino Trail**: A more challenging route offering spectacular views, covering 45 km with 1,200 m of elevation gain.

These trails are an invitation to explore pristine nature and enjoy unforgettable views.

### My Personal Experience: Circumnavigating the Lake by E-Bike

I recently tackled a unique e-bike route, with bikes rented from [Bike3Lands Shop](https://www.bike3lands.com/) in Idro, run by Davide. This circuit, which traces the shoreline, climbs toward Passo Maniva and descends to Ponte Caffaro, is an experience accessible to everyone, rich with stunning panoramas and fun.
A two-wheeled adventure that combined sport, nature, and the pleasure of a mountain refuge lunch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What are the best MTB trails at Lake Idro?**
The main routes are the Lake Route (30km, easy, scenic) and the Monte Stino Trail (45km, challenging, 1,200m elevation gain). Full details at [Greenway Valli Resilienti](https://www.greenwayvalliresilienti.it/it/percorsi/).

**Can I rent e-bikes at Lake Idro?**
Yes, [Bike3Lands Shop](https://www.bike3lands.com/) in Idro, run by Davide, offers high-quality e-bike rentals. The lake loop with the Passo Maniva climb is a recommended experience.

**Is Lake Idro good for beginner cyclists?**
Definitely. The route around the lake is mostly flat and scenic, ideal for all levels. For more challenge, there are climbs toward Passo Maniva and mountain trails.
